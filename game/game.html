Skirmish

Take the ground around the memorial and hold it for three rounds — or put the other squad down. Sixteen rounds, then it goes on objective score.

AP you don't spend is AP you keep. Anyone left with enough for a shot will fire on an enemy that moves across their line of fire. Walking a squaddie through open ground in front of four held rifles is how you lose them.

Cover is read off the edge a shot crosses, so the side you shoot from decides what it's worth. Catch someone with no cover facing you and it's a flank — half again the damage.
